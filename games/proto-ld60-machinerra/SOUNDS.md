# Sound sources

The recorded sound effects are CC0 (public domain) cuts from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), shared with Wildhold (its `SOUNDS.md` says how they were cut); no credit is required, they are listed so a sound can be traced or swapped. Crumble is cut from rise2 and pick3, snap from snap (see Wildhold's notes). The machine noises (steam bolts, the hornets' hum, oil glugs, the lift, the Oracle's tones) are synthesised in `src/sound.ts`. The music is "Rimti darbai" by Laimonas.
